Methodology and coverage. CFS (consolidated) is preferred; OFS (separate) is used when the consolidated statement is unavailable. Always compare the “FS” field before interpreting trends. OpenDART documents the standardized full-statement endpoint from 2015 onward, so 2010–2014 are flagged as unavailable through that endpoint and are not filled with zeros. Gross profit may be derived as revenue less cost of sales if no matching reported line is found. EBITDA is shown only when operating profit and a mapped depreciation/amortization item are both available. CAPEX/FCF can be incomplete if investment-outflow accounts are not standardized in the source.

Uncomputed indicators. DPO and the cash conversion cycle are left blank because a comparable credit-purchases denominator is not reliably available from standardized accounts. PER, PBR, EV/EBITDA and FCF yield require date-matched market prices, shares outstanding and/or enterprise value; these are not inferred from financial statements alone. Quick ratio uses (current assets − inventory) / current liabilities. Receivables turnover uses revenue as a proxy for credit sales.
